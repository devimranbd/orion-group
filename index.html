Scholarship for Poor & Meritorious MBBS and Postgraduate Medical Students

ORION introduced the Orion Pharma Medical Scholarship to support meritorious and underprivileged students pursuing medical education across Bangladesh for more than a decade. Through this program, several students from various reputed medical colleges receive scholarships that cover their entire courses of study, from the first year to internship. The initiative recognizes the dedication of aspiring doctors who have chosen a life of service to humanity.
