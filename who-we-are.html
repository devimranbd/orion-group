About ORION

ORION (www.orion-group.net) is a leading and distinguished conglomerate in Bangladesh carrying a legendary brand name spanning more than three decades. ORION is built on a strong foundation of determination, excellence, integrity and a firm promise to deliver value to its customers, consumers and clients. With its manufacturing facilities and operations taking place throughout the country, ORION is highly committed in bringing eminent changes in the socio-economic development in the country.

ORION is comprised of a highly diversified portfolio of Pharma & Healthcare, Infrastructure Development, Cosmetics & Toiletries, Power & Energy, Textiles & Garments, Home Appliances, Footwear & Footcare, and Agro & Consumer Products sectors.

ORION has been an integral part in the steady economic growth and socio-economic development of Bangladesh through its contribution in the Trade & Commerce sector of Bangladesh over the last four decades.
